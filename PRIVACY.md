# Privacy policy

Conexiuni Cluj for Wear OS shows live bus and tram departures in Cluj-Napoca. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking libraries.

## Location

With your permission, the app uses your location to list the stops near you and to show where you are on the map. That happens on the watch: your location is never sent anywhere.

## What the app downloads

- Stops, lines, timetables and live bus positions from [bus.bmarian.online](https://bus.bmarian.online), the server behind the Conexiuni Cluj website.
- Map tiles from [CARTO](https://carto.com/), whose [privacy policy](https://carto.com/privacy) applies to those requests.

Like any website, these servers see your IP address when the app connects to them. bus.bmarian.online doesn't store it: it keeps a salted one-way hash of it, which it uses to count daily users and how often stops and lines are opened.

## Favorites

Your favorite stops and lines are stored on the watch.

When you copy favorites from another device, that device sends them to the watch through bus.bmarian.online. If you scanned the code, they are encrypted with a key the server never sees. If you typed the code, the server can read them while it passes them on. They're only ever kept in the server's memory, never written to disk. The watch deletes them as soon as it picks them up, and an unused code expires after 10 minutes.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects nothing about anyone.
